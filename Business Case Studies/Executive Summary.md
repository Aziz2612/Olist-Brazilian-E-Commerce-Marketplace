# Executive Summary

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-commerce Marketplace using SQL to evaluate business performance across sales, customers, sellers, product categories, and logistics.

The objective was not only to calculate business KPIs, but also to uncover actionable insights and provide strategic recommendations that could support sustainable marketplace growth.

---

# Business Objectives

- Measure marketplace growth.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Evaluate customer retention.
- Analyze seller performance and revenue concentration.
- Identify the best-performing product categories.
- Assess logistics performance and customer satisfaction.
- Generate data-driven business recommendations.

---

# Executive Dashboard

The analysis covered five key business areas:

- Sales Performance
- Customer Analysis
- Seller Analysis
- Product Category Analysis
- Logistics Performance

# Key Business Insights

### 1. Marketplace Growth

The marketplace experienced strong and consistent growth throughout the analyzed period, with both orders and GMV increasing steadily.

---

### 2. Growth Was Acquisition-Driven

Business growth was primarily fueled by acquiring new customers rather than increasing spending from existing customers.

---

### 3. Customer Retention Is the Largest Business Opportunity

Approximately **97% of customers placed only one order**, highlighting significant challenges in customer retention and repeat purchasing.

---

### 4. Customer Loyalty Is Low

Cohort analysis showed that customer retention declines sharply after the first purchase, with only a small percentage of customers returning in later months.

---

### 5. Revenue Is Highly Concentrated Among Sellers

The **top 20% of sellers generate approximately 82% of total marketplace GMV**, indicating a strong Pareto distribution.

---

### 6. Seller Performance Varies Significantly

A small number of elite sellers drive marketplace performance, while the majority of sellers generate relatively low revenue.

---

### 7. Category Performance Is Uneven

Some categories generate high order volumes, while others achieve stronger revenue through higher Average Order Value (AOV).

---

### 8. Delivery Speed Influences Customer Satisfaction

Customer review scores consistently decline as delivery time increases, demonstrating a strong relationship between logistics performance and customer experience.

---

### 9. Peak Demand Creates Operational Pressure

During periods of rapid sales growth, delivery times increased and customer satisfaction declined, suggesting operational capacity constraints.

---

### 10. Sustainable Growth Requires Operational Excellence

Long-term marketplace success depends not only on acquiring customers but also on improving retention, logistics, and seller productivity.

---

# Strategic Recommendations

## Customer

- Launch loyalty and rewards programs.
- Improve post-purchase engagement.
- Increase repeat purchase rate through personalized marketing.
- Track Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) and Repeat Purchase Rate.

---

## Sellers

- Strengthen relationships with top-performing sellers.
- Support mid-tier sellers through education and performance programs.
- Diversify marketplace revenue by developing smaller sellers.

---

## Product Categories

- Increase investment in high-performing categories.
- Expand seller acquisition in underserved categories.
- Improve Average Order Value through bundles and cross-selling.

---

## Logistics

- Reduce delivery time for delayed shipments.
- Improve warehouse allocation and carrier performance.
- Prepare logistics capacity before major promotional events.
- Monitor delivery KPIs alongside customer satisfaction metrics.

---

# Final Business Assessment

The Olist marketplace demonstrates strong acquisition capabilities and healthy marketplace growth. However, the analysis reveals that long-term business performance is constrained by low customer retention, high seller revenue concentration, and operational challenges during peak demand periods.

The greatest opportunity for sustainable growth lies in improving customer lifetime value, increasing repeat purchases, strengthening seller development, and continuously optimizing logistics operations.

By addressing these areas, the marketplace can generate higher revenue, improve customer satisfaction, and build a more resilient and scalable business model.
