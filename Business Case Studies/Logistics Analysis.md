# Logistics Analysis

## Objective

Evaluate logistics performance by analyzing delivery times and their impact on customer satisfaction. The goal is to identify operational bottlenecks and recommend improvements to enhance the customer experience.

---

# 1. Delivery Performance

### KPIs

- Average Delivery Time
- Delivery Time Distribution
- Delivery Buckets
- Percentage of Orders per Bucket

### Key Insights

- The majority of orders were delivered within two weeks, indicating generally efficient logistics operations.
- Delivery times varied significantly across orders, with a small percentage taking more than 20 days.
- Long delivery times represent operational exceptions that can negatively affect customer experience.
- Most customers receive their orders within an acceptable delivery window.

### Business Recommendations

- Continue monitoring delivery lead time as a core operational KPI.
- Investigate orders exceeding 20 days to identify root causes.
- Improve carrier selection and warehouse allocation for delayed shipments.

---

# 2. Delivery Time vs Customer Satisfaction

### KPIs

- Average Review Score
- Average Review Score by Delivery Bucket

### Key Insights

- Customer satisfaction decreases as delivery time increases.
- Orders delivered within the shortest delivery windows consistently receive the highest review scores.
- Orders taking more than 20 days receive the lowest customer ratings.
- Delivery speed is one of the strongest operational drivers of customer satisfaction.

### Business Recommendations

- Prioritize reducing delivery time for slow shipments.
- Set Service Level Agreements (SLAs) with logistics partners.
- Monitor review scores alongside delivery KPIs to detect operational issues early.

---

# 3. Operational Risk

### Key Insights

- Peak sales periods place significant pressure on logistics operations.
- During months with the highest order volume, average delivery time increased while customer satisfaction declined.
- Operational capacity should scale with marketplace growth to maintain service quality.

### Business Recommendations

- Increase fulfillment capacity before major promotional events.
- Forecast demand to optimize staffing and inventory allocation.
- Continuously monitor delivery performance during peak seasons.

---

# Overall Logistics Insights

## Strengths

- Most orders are delivered within a reasonable timeframe.
- Customer satisfaction remains generally high.
- Logistics operations successfully support marketplace growth.

## Weaknesses

- Long delivery times significantly reduce customer satisfaction.
- Peak demand creates operational bottlenecks.
- Delivery performance varies across orders and product categories.

---

# Final Business Conclusion

Logistics performance is a critical driver of customer satisfaction on the Olist marketplace. Although most orders are delivered within acceptable timeframes, prolonged deliveries have a clear negative impact on review scores and overall customer experience. As the marketplace continues to grow, investing in logistics capacity, carrier performance, and delivery optimization will be essential to sustaining customer satisfaction and supporting long-term business growth.
