# Category Analysis

## Objective

Evaluate product category performance to identify the categories driving marketplace growth, compare category efficiency, and uncover opportunities to optimize product assortment and seller performance.

---

# 1. Category Sales Performance

### KPIs

- Orders
- GMV
- GMV Contribution %
- AOV (Average Order Value)

### Key Insights

- **Beleza_Saude** generated the highest GMV, contributing over **9%** of the marketplace revenue.
- **Relogios_Presentes** ranked among the highest revenue-generating categories despite having fewer orders than several other categories.
- **Cama_Mesa_Banho** generated the largest number of orders but ranked second in GMV.
- High order volume does not always translate into the highest revenue, highlighting the importance of Average Order Value (AOV).

### Business Recommendations

- Continue investing in the highest-GMV categories.
- Prioritize marketing campaigns for categories combining high order volume with high GMV.
- Identify categories with strong order volume but relatively low AOV and increase basket value through bundles and cross-selling.

---

# 2. Average Order Value (AOV)

### KPI

Average Order Value by Category

### Key Insights

- Significant differences exist in AOV across product categories.
- Categories such as **Instrumentos_Musicais**, **Moveis_Escritorio**, and **Relogios_Presentes** generate considerably higher revenue per order.
- Categories with low AOV depend heavily on high transaction volume to generate revenue.

### Business Recommendations

- Increase exposure for high-AOV categories through premium placement.
- Introduce complementary product recommendations to increase basket size.
- Bundle low-priced products with related items to improve AOV.

---

# 3. Category Market Competition

### KPIs

- Unique Sellers
- GMV per Seller
- Orders per Seller

### Key Insights

- Competition varies substantially between categories.
- Categories with many sellers generally generate lower average revenue per seller.
- **Relogios_Presentes** stands out by combining high GMV with relatively few sellers, resulting in exceptionally high revenue per seller.
- Some categories appear highly saturated, while others offer attractive opportunities for seller growth.

### Business Recommendations

- Encourage new sellers to join underserved, high-performing categories.
- Reduce oversaturation in highly competitive categories.
- Use seller performance benchmarks to identify expansion opportunities.

---

# 4. Customer Satisfaction

### KPIs

- Average Review Score
- Average Delivery Time

### Key Insights

- Most categories maintain customer ratings close to **4 out of 5**, indicating generally high customer satisfaction.
- Delivery time differs across categories.
- Large or bulky products typically require longer delivery times.
- Categories with longer delivery times tend to receive slightly lower customer ratings.

### Business Recommendations

- Optimize logistics for categories with slow delivery.
- Prioritize faster shipping for large-volume categories.
- Monitor customer reviews to identify operational issues before they impact demand.

---

# 5. Category Efficiency

Instead of evaluating categories only by sales volume, combining multiple KPIs provides a better view of category performance.

High-performing categories typically demonstrate:

- High GMV
- Strong AOV
- Healthy seller productivity
- High customer ratings
- Reasonable delivery times

### Business Recommendations

- Expand investment in categories that perform well across multiple KPIs.
- Improve operational efficiency before increasing marketing spend on underperforming categories.
- Monitor category performance using a balanced scorecard rather than GMV alone.

---

# Overall Category Insights

## Strengths

- Several categories generate consistently strong GMV.
- Customer satisfaction is generally high across the marketplace.
- High-value categories create significant revenue opportunities.

## Weaknesses

- Seller competition is uneven across categories.
- Some categories generate many orders but relatively low revenue.
- Delivery performance varies considerably between categories.

---

# Final Business Conclusion

Category performance varies significantly across the marketplace. While some categories excel in sales volume, others generate higher revenue through stronger Average Order Value and greater seller productivity. The analysis suggests that marketplace growth should focus not only on increasing order volume but also on improving category efficiency, optimizing seller distribution, increasing basket value, and enhancing the customer experience through faster delivery and higher service quality.
