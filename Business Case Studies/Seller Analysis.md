# Seller Analysis

## Objective

Analyze seller performance, marketplace concentration, and revenue distribution to identify how GMV is generated and evaluate the dependency on top-performing sellers.

---

# 1. Seller Growth

### KPI
- Monthly Active Sellers

### Key Insights

- The number of active sellers increased steadily throughout the analyzed period.
- Active sellers grew from fewer than 250 sellers in early 2017 to more than 1,200 sellers by mid-2018.
- Seller growth closely followed customer and order growth, indicating healthy marketplace expansion.
- The platform continued attracting new merchants while maintaining existing sellers.

### Business Recommendations

- Continue expanding seller acquisition programs.
- Improve seller onboarding to accelerate first sales.
- Monitor monthly seller activation and retention rates.

---

# 2. Seller Revenue Distribution

### KPIs

- Total Sellers
- Average Revenue per Seller
- Median Revenue per Seller
- Maximum Seller Revenue
- Minimum Seller Revenue

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------:|
| Total Sellers | 3,053 |
| Average Revenue per Seller | $5,154.12 |
| Median Revenue per Seller | $1,013.59 |
| Maximum Seller Revenue | $249,393.44 |
| Minimum Seller Revenue | $12.22 |

### Key Insights

- Revenue distribution is highly skewed.
- The average seller generates over $5K, while the median seller generates only about $1K.
- This large gap indicates that a small number of sellers generate exceptionally high revenue while most sellers remain relatively small.
- Seller performance varies significantly across the marketplace.

### Business Recommendations

- Provide educational resources for low-performing sellers.
- Share best practices from top-performing sellers.
- Offer seller performance dashboards and benchmarking tools.

---

# 3. Seller Segmentation

### KPI

Revenue-Based Seller Segments

- Tier 1 – Elite (Top 10%)
- Tier 2 – Mid-Tier (Next 30%)
- Tier 3 – Low-Volume (Bottom 60%)

### Key Insights

- Seller performance is highly concentrated among a relatively small group of merchants.
- Elite sellers contribute a disproportionately large share of marketplace GMV.
- Most sellers belong to the low-volume segment and generate relatively small revenue.
- The marketplace relies heavily on its highest-performing sellers.

### Business Recommendations

- Develop exclusive programs for elite sellers.
- Identify high-potential mid-tier sellers and support their growth.
- Improve seller education and marketing support for low-volume sellers.

---

# 4. GMV Concentration

### KPIs

| Group | GMV Contribution |
|------|----------------:|
| Top 10 Sellers | 12.86% |
| Top 50 Sellers | 32.36% |
| Top 100 Sellers | 44.56% |
| Top 20% Sellers | 81.93% |

### Key Insights

- The **Top 10 sellers alone generate nearly 13% of total GMV**.
- The **Top 50 sellers contribute almost one-third of marketplace revenue**.
- The **Top 100 sellers generate nearly half of total GMV**.
- The **Top 20% of sellers account for more than 80% of total GMV**, demonstrating a clear Pareto distribution.
- Marketplace revenue is highly concentrated among a relatively small number of sellers.

### Business Recommendations

- Protect relationships with top-performing sellers through dedicated account management.
- Reduce business risk by helping mid-sized sellers grow.
- Diversify revenue sources to reduce dependence on a small seller base.

---

# Overall Seller Insights

## Strengths

- Strong seller growth.
- Healthy marketplace expansion.
- High-performing sellers generate substantial GMV.

## Weaknesses

- Revenue is heavily concentrated among a small number of sellers.
- Most sellers contribute relatively little revenue.
- Marketplace performance depends significantly on elite sellers.

---

# Final Business Conclusion

Olist has successfully expanded its seller ecosystem, with the number of active sellers growing consistently over time. However, seller revenue follows a highly concentrated distribution, where a small percentage of merchants generate the majority of marketplace GMV. Supporting mid-tier sellers, improving low-performing seller productivity, and reducing dependence on elite sellers represent significant opportunities to build a more balanced and resilient marketplace.
