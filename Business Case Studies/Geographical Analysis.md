# 🌍 Geographical Analysis

## Objective

Analyze marketplace performance across Brazilian states to identify regional differences in sales performance, customer behavior, logistics efficiency, and seller distribution.

This analysis answers the following business questions:

- Which states generate the highest GMV?
- Where are the largest customer bases located?
- Which regions provide the best customer experience?
- How does delivery performance vary across Brazil?
- Which states present the strongest growth opportunities?

---

# Dataset Used

The analysis combines data from:

- Orders
- Order Items
- Customers
- Reviews

Cancelled and unavailable orders were excluded from all calculations.

---

# KPIs Analyzed

- Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Active Sellers
- GMV per Seller
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Average Delivery Days
- Average Review Score

---

# Key Insights

## 1. São Paulo (SP) is the Marketplace's Economic Hub

São Paulo dominates every major business metric.

**Performance**

- GMV: **$5.88M**
- Orders: **41,125**
- Customers: **39,747**
- Active Sellers: **2,510**
- Average Delivery Time: **8.72 days**
- Average Review Score: **4.14 / 5**

### Business Insight

São Paulo serves as the marketplace's primary commercial hub, supported by the highest customer concentration, the largest seller network, and one of the fastest delivery operations.

Its combination of scale and operational efficiency makes it the most valuable region in the business.

---

## 2. Rio de Janeiro Generates Strong Sales Despite Weaker Customer Experience

Rio de Janeiro ranks second in overall GMV but underperforms operationally.

| Metric | São Paulo | Rio de Janeiro |
|---------|----------:|---------------:|
| GMV | $5.88M | $2.12M |
| Average Delivery | 8.72 Days | 15.15 Days |
| Review Score | 4.14 | 3.82 |

### Business Insight

Demand remains strong in Rio de Janeiro despite slower deliveries and lower customer satisfaction.

Improving fulfillment performance in RJ represents one of the highest-impact operational opportunities.

---

## 3. Delivery Performance Declines in Northern States

Several northern states experience significantly longer delivery times.

| State | Avg Delivery |
|--------|-------------:|
| AP | 28.21 Days |
| RR | 28.23 Days |
| AM | 26.39 Days |
| PA | 23.75 Days |

These states also tend to receive lower customer review scores.

### Business Insight

Long transportation distances and lower seller density appear to negatively affect customer experience.

Delivery speed is a key driver of customer satisfaction across regions.

---

## 4. Smaller Markets Generate Higher Average Order Values

Although several states contribute relatively small GMV, they generate some of the highest Average Order Values.

| State | AOV |
|--------|----:|
| PB | $264.64 |
| AC | $242.84 |
| AP | $239.16 |
| RO | $233.43 |
| AL | $234.13 |

### Business Insight

Customers in these regions tend to spend more per transaction, making them attractive markets for premium products, bundles, and high-value categories.

---

## 5. Seller Distribution is Highly Concentrated

Seller activity is heavily concentrated in southeastern Brazil.

São Paulo alone hosts more than **2,500 active sellers**, while many northern states have only a few hundred sellers.

### Business Insight

This uneven seller distribution likely contributes to:

- Faster deliveries in major markets
- Longer shipping times in remote regions
- Lower customer satisfaction outside seller hubs

Expanding seller coverage in underserved regions could significantly improve nationwide logistics performance.

---

## 6. Southern States Deliver Balanced Performance

States including:

- Minas Gerais (MG)
- Paraná (PR)
- Santa Catarina (SC)

consistently demonstrate:

- Strong GMV
- Healthy order volume
- Good customer ratings
- Efficient delivery performance

### Business Insight

These regions represent stable, mature markets with strong operational performance and attractive opportunities for continued expansion.

---

# Business Recommendations

### Expand Seller Acquisition

Increase seller onboarding efforts in northern and northeastern Brazil to improve regional coverage and reduce shipping distances.

---

### Invest in Regional Fulfillment Centers

Establishing additional fulfillment hubs outside São Paulo would reduce delivery times and improve customer satisfaction across remote regions.

---

### Improve Logistics in Rio de Janeiro

Rio de Janeiro already has strong customer demand.

Reducing delivery times should increase review scores, customer retention, and repeat purchases.

---

### Promote Premium Products in High-AOV Markets

Focus premium assortments and bundled offers in states where customers consistently spend more per order.

---

### Maintain Leadership in São Paulo

Continue investing in logistics, seller acquisition, and customer experience to protect the marketplace's largest revenue-generating region.

---

# Conclusion

Marketplace performance varies considerably across Brazilian states.

São Paulo remains the company's strongest market, combining high demand with operational excellence. Meanwhile, several underserved regions present meaningful growth opportunities through seller expansion and logistics improvements.

Reducing regional disparities in delivery performance and expanding seller coverage would strengthen customer satisfaction and support sustainable marketplace growth nationwide.
