# Sales Performance Analysis

## Business Questions

This analysis aims to answer the following business questions:

- How has the marketplace grown over time?
- Is revenue growth driven by customer acquisition or higher customer spending?
- How do customer satisfaction and delivery performance evolve over time?
- Which operational challenges emerged during periods of rapid growth?

---

## KPIs

- Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)
- Total Orders
- Active Customers
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Average Review Score
- Average Delivery Time

---

# Key Findings

## Insight 1 — Strong Marketplace Growth Throughout 2017

The marketplace experienced consistent month-over-month growth during 2017, with both order volume and GMV increasing steadily.

The most significant growth occurred in **November 2017**, where the business reached its highest monthly sales volume during the analyzed period.

### Business Impact

The marketplace successfully attracted a large number of new customers and significantly expanded transaction volume.

---

## Insight 2 — Growth Was Primarily Customer-Driven

Although GMV increased sharply during the peak sales months, the Average Order Value (AOV) declined.

This indicates that revenue growth was mainly driven by an increase in the number of purchasing customers rather than higher spending per order.

### Business Impact

Customer acquisition campaigns were highly successful, but they encouraged smaller basket sizes.

---

## Insight 3 — Customer Experience Declined During Peak Demand

During November 2017, the marketplace recorded:

- The highest number of orders
- The highest average delivery time
- The lowest average review score

These metrics suggest that operational capacity struggled to keep pace with the surge in demand.

### Business Impact

Rapid growth placed pressure on logistics and fulfillment operations, leading to a temporary decline in customer satisfaction.

---

## Insight 4 — Delivery Performance Directly Influenced Customer Satisfaction

Across the monthly trend, months with longer delivery times consistently showed lower customer review scores.

This indicates a strong relationship between logistics performance and overall customer experience.

### Business Impact

Improving delivery efficiency is likely to have a direct positive impact on customer satisfaction.

---

# Business Recommendations

### 1. Prepare operational capacity before major sales events.

Forecast demand and increase warehouse and shipping capacity before promotional periods.

---

### 2. Focus on increasing Average Order Value.

Introduce product bundles, upselling, and cross-selling campaigns instead of relying solely on customer acquisition.

---

### 3. Closely monitor logistics KPIs.

Track delivery lead time during high-demand periods to prevent customer dissatisfaction.

---

### 4. Balance growth with operational excellence.

Sustainable marketplace growth requires scaling logistics capabilities alongside increasing order volume.
