# 🛒 Olist E-Commerce Marketplace Analysis (SQL)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-commerce Marketplace using SQL to uncover business insights across customers, sellers, products, sales, and marketplace performance.

The objective is not only to write SQL queries but also to answer real business questions and provide actionable recommendations that could help marketplace managers improve growth, customer retention, seller performance, and operational efficiency.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall marketplace performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Measure customer retention and repeat purchases.
- Evaluate seller performance and marketplace concentration.
- Analyze product category performance.
- Identify growth opportunities through business insights.
- Provide data-driven recommendations.

---

## 🗂 Dataset

The project uses the **Olist Brazilian E-commerce Public Dataset**, which contains information about:

- Orders
- Customers
- Sellers
- Products

# 📈 Key Business Insights

- A small percentage of sellers generates the majority of marketplace revenue.
- Customer retention remains relatively low, indicating an opportunity to improve repeat purchases.
- Some product categories generate high revenue despite having relatively few sellers.
- Premium categories achieve significantly higher Average Order Values.
- Delivery performance varies considerably across product categories.
- Marketplace revenue is concentrated within a limited number of product categories.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase investment in high-performing product categories.
- Improve customer retention through loyalty and remarketing campaigns.
- Recruit more sellers into high-demand, low-competition categories.
- Reduce delivery times for slow-performing categories.
- Monitor seller concentration to reduce marketplace dependency on top sellers.
